# Gym Validation Sheet — print this, take it to 5 gyms

**Do not sell anything today.** You are only allowed to ask questions and listen. The moment you pitch, you lose the honest answer.

- Each conversation: **12–15 minutes**
- Best time to visit: **11am–1pm** (gyms are quiet) or **4–6pm**
- Bring: this sheet, a pen, your phone with `index.html` open
- Target: 5 gyms. 3 local independents, 1 mid-size (100+ members), 1 small (<50 members)

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

> "Hi, I'm building a small tool for gym owners and I'm not selling anything. I just want to understand how you handle member renewals. Can I ask you 5 quick questions? 10 minutes."

If they say no → "No problem, can I come back on a weekday morning?" Then go to the next gym.

---

## The 5 questions

> **Ask exactly these. Write down their exact words — not your summary.**

### Q1
**"How do you currently know which members are about to expire?"**

Follow-ups:
- "Where is that written down?"
- "Who checks it?"
- "How often do you actually look at it?"

Look for: register/notebook, Excel, an app, or *"I just remember."*

### Q2 ⭐ (the money question)
**"Last month, how many members stopped coming without telling you why? Can you name one?"**

Follow-ups:
- "Did you call them?"
- "Do you know why they left?"
- "What did that member pay per month?"

Look for: hesitation, a specific name, or *"I don't really know."* **This answer decides everything.**

### Q3
**"How do you remind people to renew? What happens on the day it expires?"**

Follow-ups:
- "Do you message them or do they message you?"
- "How many reminders before you give up?"
- "Do you have their WhatsApp number?"

Look for: *"they usually come themselves"* = they're losing people.

### Q4
**"What do you use today — register, Excel, an app?"**

Follow-ups:
- "What do you like about it?"
- "What's the most annoying part?"
- "When did you last open it?"

Look for: an existing app (bad sign) vs. paper (good sign). If they use an app, ask what it costs and whether they'd switch.

### Q5 (pricing signal — ask last)
**"If a tool saved you two renewals a month and auto-sent the WhatsApp reminders, what would that be worth to you per month?"**

Follow-ups:
- "Would ₹999/month feel fair, too much, or cheap?"
- "Would you pay yearly if it was 2 months free?"

**Do not defend the price. Just write the number and their face.**

## Decision rules

**BUILD IT if:**
- 3 of 5 track expiry in a **register or by memory** (not software), AND
- 3 of 5 can name a member they **lost without knowing why**, AND
- average price they named is **≥ ₹500/month**

**PAUSE if:**
- They track it manually but can't name a lost member → pain is real but not felt. Your pitch won't land.
- Price named is ₹200–400 → you'd need 500 gyms. Not worth it solo.

**WALK AWAY if:**
- 3 of 5 already use an app they're **happy** with → you're too late in this area. Try a different city or a different vertical (physio clinics, martial arts, dance classes, swimming pools, tuitions).

## The demo (only after all 5 questions)

Now show `index.html` on your phone. **Do not explain the features.** Say only:

> "This is a rough version. This red number is money sitting in expired memberships right now."

Then shut up and watch. Their reaction is your real validation:

- Leans in / asks "can it do X" → **strong signal**
- "Nice" and hands it back → **polite no**
- "I don't have time for another app" → **listen to that, it's the real objection**

Then close with exactly one question:

> "If I set this up with your actual member list and it worked, would you want it this month?"

Write down yes / maybe / no. Nothing else counts.

## Objections you will hear, and what to say

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I keep it in a register, it works fine" | "How many members did you lose last month without knowing why?" (return to Q2) |
| "Khatabook is free" | "Khatabook tracks money owed. This tells you whose membership is about to end — different job." |
| "I don't have time to enter data" | "I'll enter your first 50 members for you. Takes me 20 minutes, not you." |
| "My members come back on their own" | "Then the red number should be zero. Can we check?" |
| "Too expensive" | "One renewal at ₹1,200 covers the whole year. How many do you lose a month?" |
| "Send me details on WhatsApp" | "Sure." — then message them the same day. This is a real lead. |

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't show the demo before asking all 5 questions
- ❌ Don't argue with their current method
- ❌ Don't offer a free trial on day one — it signals low confidence
- ❌ Don't add features they mention mid-conversation (write them down, stay quiet)
- ❌ Don't visit more than 5 gyms before deciding. You'll just collect noise.
